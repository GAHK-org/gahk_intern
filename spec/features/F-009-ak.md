# Feature: AK-krydser — tracking the labour every resident owes the house

**Feature ID:** F-009

## What it is

AK (aktivitetskrydser) is the kollegium's labour bookkeeping. Each month every resident owes some
hours of work for the house, booked as a deduction of krydser (crosses; 2 by default). Doing the work
earns krydser back. The running balance may go negative — that means behind — and the goal is to
stay at 0 or above. Residents see their own balance and log, and can register work they have done.
The AK embedsgruppe (role `ak`) sees everyone, adjusts anyone's krydser, and sets how many krydser
each calendar month costs.

All of it lives in `app/ak/`, mounted under `/intern/ak/` (`app/residents/urls.py`), namespace `ak:`.
The sidebar always shows "AK-krydser" and, for role `ak`, "AK-oversigt"
(`app/core/context_processors.py`). The AK balance also shows up in Regnskab
(`app/residents/views_regnskab.py` reads `AkEntry.balance_for`).

**What it replaces.** The PHP version kept a running-total `status` column next to a log, with a
bulk "reduce everyone" button that an officer had to press each period. Here the balance is the sum
of a ledger, and the monthly deduction books itself.

## Data model

`app/ak/models.py`.

- **`AkEntry`** — one change to one resident's krydser: `delta` (signed integer), `kind`, `reason`,
  `created_at`, `created_by` (null for system, monthly and migrated rows). Kinds: `monthly`
  ("Månedlig afregning"), `labour` ("Udført arbejde"), `adjustment` ("Manuel justering"), `opening`
  ("Startsaldo (migreret)", the migrated legacy balance). A `monthly` row also carries the `year`
  and `month` it belongs to. **A conditional unique constraint allows at most one `monthly` row per
  resident per (year, month).** The balance is `AkEntry.balance_for(resident)`, the sum of `delta`.
- **`AkMonthlyCharge`** — the schedule: one row per calendar month 1–12 with `krydser` (a positive
  number to subtract) and `active`. It is keyed on the calendar month, so January costs the same
  every year. Migration `0002` seeds all 12 rows as active at 2 krydser.
- **`AkAutoApply`** — single row (`pk=1`) recording the last (year, month) whose deduction was
  applied automatically.

## The monthly deduction

"Month" here means the **active period** (`residents.models.active_period`): the newest published
alumneliste whose month has started, falling back to the calendar month if there is none. A list
prepared for next month does not count until that month arrives.

`ak.services.apply_monthly_charge(year, month)` makes the ledger match the schedule for one period,
in one transaction:

- if that calendar month's schedule row is missing or inactive, **every** `monthly` entry for the
  period is deleted;
- otherwise, anyone who is not on that month's alumneliste (`Residency` rows) loses their `monthly`
  entry for the period, and every resident on it gets exactly one, with `delta = -krydser` and the
  reason "Månedlig vurdering YYYY-MM" (`update_or_create`, so an amount change updates the row).

It is idempotent and returns (written, removed). Residents who are not on the list are never charged.

Three things call it:

1. **The scheduled task**, `ak.tasks.apply_monthly_assessment` → `manage.py ak_monthly_assessment`,
   which creates a default schedule row (active, 2) for the active month if one is missing, then
   applies the active period.
2. **The lazy backstop**, `ak.services.ensure_active_month_applied`, called on the internal
   dashboard (`residents.views.dashboard`), `my_ak` and `overview`. If `AkAutoApply` already holds the
   active period, it does nothing (one cheap lookup). Otherwise it applies the period and then
   advances the marker. A failure is logged, not raised, and leaves the marker alone, so the next
   page load retries.
3. **Saving the schedule** (`save_monthly_charges`) writes all 12 rows — an unchecked month becomes
   inactive, and a non-numeric amount becomes 2 — with a minimum of 1 krydser, and re-applies **only
   the active period**, with the officer recorded as `created_by`. Earlier months are never touched.

## Logging work

- **Residents** (`add_self_entry`): a whole number of krydser ≥ 1 and a non-blank description are
  required. The entry is `labour` with `created_by` = the resident. It counts right away; there is no
  approval step.
- **Officers** (`add_entry`): any non-zero integer (`+2` parses). Positive becomes `labour`, negative
  becomes `adjustment`. The note is optional. Zero or non-numeric input is silently ignored.
- **Officers delete** (`delete_entry`): hard-deletes the row, any kind, scoped to the resident in the
  URL.

AK-oversigt lists residents on the active period's alumneliste, lowest balance first, each linking
to their log.

## Scheduled jobs

| Job | When | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ak.tasks.apply_monthly_assessment` (Celery beat, `apply-ak-monthly-assessment`) | 1st of the month, 04:10 | Applies the active period's deduction |

At 04:10 on the 1st, the active period only moves on if the new month's alumneliste is already
published. If it is published later, the lazy backstop books it on the first internal page load
after that.

## Decisions

- **An append-only ledger instead of a running total** (`models.py` docstring). The balance is a
  `SUM`, so it is always consistent, and the monthly charge, labour and adjustments are all just
  rows. It replaces the legacy column and its buggy bulk operations.
- **`year`/`month` on the monthly row is the authoritative period key, not `created_at`**, so
  re-running a month can never double-charge. The unique constraint backs this up in the database.
  Migration `0002` filled in the key for legacy monthly rows from their "YYYY-MM" reason tag.
- **The schedule is per calendar month and the same every year** (`AkMonthlyCharge` docstring), so
  officers set it once rather than every period.
- **Cron plus a lazy backstop** (`AkAutoApply`, `ensure_active_month_applied` docstrings). Cron
  fails silently and a skipped month leaves every balance wrong, so the first internal page load of a
  new period does the same job. Whichever comes first wins, and the marker makes the hot path cheap.
- **Saving the schedule re-books only the active month**, so balances already settled stay as they
  are (`save_monthly_charges` docstring).

## Open questions

- **The ledger is not append-only in practice.** `delete_entry` hard-deletes any row, with no confirm
  dialog, no audit record of who deleted what, and no restriction on kind. `apply_monthly_charge`
  also deletes and rewrites `monthly` rows. A deleted monthly row for the active period comes back
  the next time the schedule is saved; one for a past period does not.
- **Self-reported labour is unreviewed.** `add_self_entry`'s docstring says officers "review/adjust
  via the overview", but there is no pending state: any resident can give themselves any number of
  krydser immediately. The only check is an officer reading the log.
- **Saving the schedule overwrites `created_by`** on every active-period `monthly` row with the
  saving officer, including rows the cron wrote earlier.
- **Residents can change during a month.** Re-applying the active period after someone was removed
  from the alumneliste deletes their charge for that month, which may or may not be intended for a
  mid-month move-out.
- **The backstop and the command treat a missing schedule row differently**: the command creates a
  default (active, 2) row, the backstop removes all charges for the period. With all 12 rows seeded
  this only matters if a row is deleted by hand.
- **Officers' empty or zero input does nothing and says nothing** (`add_entry` shows no message).
