# Feature: Den Hurtige — the quick-message chat

The kollegium's chat for things relevant in the next hours, in fixed channels, replacing the
Messenger group. Messages leave the feed when their time runs out and are kept in the archive.

## Audit trail

- Writing a message, replying, and deleting a message are in the audit trail (audit-trail.md), with
  what was written. A moderator deleting somebody's message is recorded as the moderator's doing.
- A message deleted after its first five minutes becomes «Besked slettet» on the feed; the trail
  keeps what it said, for administrators, for the trail's own time. That is the record of who wrote
  what, also when it has been taken back.
- Left out: reactions (as everywhere), and a message leaving the feed for the archive, which happens
  by the clock and changes nothing.
