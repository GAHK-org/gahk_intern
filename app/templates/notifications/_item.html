{% comment %} One notification: a link, and its own buttons. Shared by the bell's dropdown and the notifications page; the dropdown includes it `with in_dropdown=True`. The link goes through notifications:open, which marks it seen and then redirects to where it points — "seen" means clicked or marked as read, in-app or on the push, never merely listed. The buttons post with htmx and come back through `next`: in the dropdown that is the dropdown's own URL, swapped into #notif-panel; on the page it is the page itself, of which only #notif-content is swapped in, so the reader keeps their place. Without JavaScript the page's buttons still work as plain forms. Only the buttons that would change something are shown: no "read" on a read row, and on the "Færdige" tab only the way back. Importance shows twice on purpose: as a coloured edge (notif-imp-N, readable at a glance in a list) and, for Høj and Kritisk, as a word — colour alone says nothing to a colour-blind reader. {% endcomment %}
