{% comment %} Amendment 4 step 1 (design doc §7): "Mine kommende vagter" plus "Vagter til overtagelse". Rendered by both koekken/index.html and the htmx swap after every offer/withdraw/take POST (koekken.views._bytte_context), so the two can never disagree. P2 §10 conventions: a refusal renders INSIDE this partial (`error`), never through the messages framework; an unavailable action's button is ABSENT, never disabled -- the predicates (services.can_offer/can_take/can_take_whole) decide per row. Amendment 4 step 2 adds trading: `upcoming` is (tildeling, flagged_by_name, open_offer_or_None, can_offer, handoff_label, incoming) tuples, where `incoming` is the live proposals on that offer as (forslag, can_accept, can_decline). `board` is (bytte, can_take, can_take_whole, proposable_rows, own_proposals) tuples, where `own_proposals` is the viewer's live open proposals on it as (forslag, can_withdraw_proposal). A select/form/button is rendered only when its predicate holds. The whole-shift hours are 2 x the shift's own duration: widthratio computes duration / 30 (and duration / 60 for the shift's own hours). {% endcomment %}
