That placement is the whole design: * #js-feed is morphed every 5s with the entire message list. A panel inside it would be rebuilt by every poll, and destroyed outright the moment its parent message expired mid-read. * #js-feed is also overflow-y:auto, so a full-screen mobile panel inside it would be clipped by and scroll with the list. Closing the panel removes this node and htmx disposes the timer with it -- there is no bookkeeping, and an empty #js-thread polls nothing at all. Two pollers on one page is deliberate.
