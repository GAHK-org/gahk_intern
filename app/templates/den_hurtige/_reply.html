{% load text_extras %} {% comment %} ONE reply in a thread, drawn as the same chat bubble a message is on the feed — the .msg classes and every rule styles.css hangs off them: the avatar at the end of a run and the name at its start, your own replies on the right in green, the photo inside the bubble and a reaction row underneath. Replies used to be a flat list of name, time and text with a rule down the side, which read as comments under a post rather than as the conversation they are. NOT _message.html with a flag. A reply has no countdown, no delete, no swipe and no thread of its own, and a message carries a branch for each of those; a second flag through that partial would have touched every one of them to switch them off again. What the two share is the drawing, and that is shared — in styles.css and in _avatar.html and _reactions.html. `reply` carries .reaction_rows, .grouped and .group_end (views._thread_replies, which runs the replies through the same mark_runs as the feed). `post` is the message it answers. `read_only` is set by the view for an archived or deleted thread: the reaction row is drawn inert, as the message's own row is, and `archived` alone cools the bubble the way the archive does. The clock is shown once per run, above its first reply, and only as a time unless the reply was written on another day than the message. The thread is one conversation, mostly within the hour; the message above it carries the full date. {% endcomment %}
